This policy explains which personal data the game HONK! Taxi Tycoon (called KORNA! in Turkish and HUPE! in German, the "Game") processes, why, and how. It is written with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698) in mind.

1. No account needed

You can play without an account, name, email address or phone number. Your progress is stored only on your device. The Game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

  • Game analytics settings, leagues, guilds and friend codes currently work on your device; nothing is sent to our servers. If we add online features, we will update this policy first.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent for notifications.
  • Your payment details (card etc.) never reach us; Google Play (or Apple) handles payment.

3. Sharing

We share data only with providers needed to run the Game: Google (Google Play billing and Google AdMob for rewarded ads — see how Google uses data from partner apps) and RevenueCat, Inc. (purchase verification, USA). International transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (GDPR Art. 46, KVKK Art. 9). We never sell your data.

4. Retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete the Game or use "Delete all my data".
  • Purchase records: as long as the law requires (e.g. tax law).
  • Advertising data: according to Google's retention policies.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR Art. 15–22 and KVKK Art. 11 you may request access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. Where processing is based on consent you can withdraw it at any time.

  • In the Game, Settings → Privacy lets you change your ad preference, open Google's ad privacy options (EU/UK) and permanently delete all data with "Delete all my data".
  • For any other request, email btrinteractive@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • You may also complain to your data protection authority.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Personalized ads are off unless you opt in. Parental approval for purchases is handled by the app store.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here and announce important changes in the Game.
